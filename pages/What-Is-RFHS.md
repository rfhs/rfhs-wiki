# What is RFHS?

The **Radio Frequency Hackers Sanctuary (RFHS)** is a group of volunteers who attend and work at cybersecurity and Hacker Conferences to run the **RF Village**, **RF Capture the Flag (RFCTF)**, and an **RF Talk Track**, depending on the conference size. RFHS has also done classes and demos in the past.

## Who we are

The organization brings together: enthusiasts, students, teachers, hackers, crackers, developers, the curious, nerds, geeks, and goobers. We want to enable a collaborative space to learn and test RF, RF technology, Protocols, Standards, and Policies. Through collaboration, we hope to learn about RF, better understand RF, help to protect RF and RF capabilities, and sometimes just have fun.

## The RF CTF

The RFCTF is designed to allow people to experience and see real-world RF capabilities and test themselves against it through competition. When discovering things like weird Wi-Fi or restaurant pagers, you need permission to “test” these things, and we enable that as part of the Game/CTF. Not all RF technologies are present or can be gamified, but we have done our best to bring a considerable amount of challenges covering a wide array of RF you may encounter.

➡️ Ready to play? See [Getting Started](RF-CTF-Virtual-HowToGetStarted) and the [Rules](RF-CTF-RULES).

## The RF Talk Track

The RF Talk Track features predominantly at DEF CON. People submit during our call for papers and we screen and approve them. We try to ensure a wide variety of topics, from basics to in-depth tech talks for those with tape in the middle of their spectacles that say “Well Actually”.

## Where to find us

We attend several conferences physically and support some virtually.
